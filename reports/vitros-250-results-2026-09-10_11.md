## Cause

The VITROS 250 numbers every result file it sends (R0001546, R0001547, ...). Over these two days it produced 158 files (R0001546 to R0001703) but the connector received only 97: **61 files are missing**, and 23 of the 24 gaps in the sequence coincide with a Kermit collision logged by the connector (`0005 INVALID PACKET USAGE`, `0008 INVALID SEQUENCE USE`, `0009 INVALID CONSTRUCTION`; 102 in total over the two days).

Kermit is half-duplex: one side transmits at a time. The connector polls HMIS every 30 s and pushes new sample programs (SFILEn.D) to the analyzer as soon as they arrive. When the analyzer has just begun sending a result file, the connector's line guard (`awaitIdle` in `src/codec/kermit/link.ts`) only checks whether *data* packets have arrived; a transfer that is still at its S/F (start) packets, or that begins during the 1 s inter-transfer pause, is not seen. The connector then sends its own S packet on top of the analyzer's transfer, the analyzer replies with an error packet and **abandons the result file; it does not resend it**. The connector's order download just retries on the next poll and succeeds, so from the connector side nothing looks wrong.

Every result file that *did* arrive was synced to HMIS (81 of 81 patient files; one of them, ZC2609090110, had assay code 32 with no order row so that single result was not filed).
